Privacy Policy for Arrow Bloom

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Arrow Bloom ("the App"), published by AW EagleCraft ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Arrow Bloom does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The App does not require an account or login, and it does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything from your device.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress — your current level and garden, stars, best combo, perfect clears, Daily Bloom results and streak, a board in progress so you can resume it, and your sound, music and haptics preferences — is stored locally on your device only (using Android SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed when you uninstall the App. On Android 13 and newer your language choice for the App is held by the Android per-app language setting.

3. Permissions

The App requests only the vibration permission, used for optional haptic feedback that you can switch off in Settings. It requests no internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

4. Sharing a result

The Share button opens the Android share sheet with a short text result that you choose to send. It is delivered only to the app you pick; we do not receive it.

5. Advertising and analytics

The current version of the App does not display third-party ads and does not use any advertising or analytics SDKs. If a future version introduces advertising or analytics, this policy will be updated and published before that version is released.

6. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any data from children. Because no personal data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

7. Third parties

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any information to third parties, because we do not collect any.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
