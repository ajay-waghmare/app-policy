Privacy Policy for Sortopia

Last updated: 19 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Sortopia ("the App"), published by AW EagleCraft ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Sortopia does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The App does not require an account, login, or internet connection to play.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress — such as your current level, best level, daily-challenge results, achievement statistics, and sound/vibration/colour-blind-aid preferences — is stored locally on your device only (using Android SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Advertising

The current version of the App does not display third-party ads and does not use any advertising or analytics SDKs. If future versions introduce advertising, this policy will be updated and published before that version is released.

4. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any data from children. Because no personal data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

5. Third parties

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any information to third parties, because we do not collect any.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
