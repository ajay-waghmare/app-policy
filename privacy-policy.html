Privacy Policy for Block Bloom

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Block Bloom ("the App"), published by AW EagleCraft ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

The App uses Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring and Cloud Messaging) to understand how the game is played and to find and fix crashes. We use this to improve the game — for example, to see where new players get stuck.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account, and the App has no login. The information Firebase collects is:

WhatExamplesWhy
Device and app identifiers A randomly generated installation ID that identifies your installation of the App, not you personally To count players and sessions without identifying anyone
Device and app information Device model and brand, Android version, app version, language To know which devices to support and test on
Approximate location Country and region, derived from your IP address. Not GPS. The App never requests location permission To know which countries to translate the game into
In-game activity Events such as starting a game, finishing a game, completing the tutorial, clearing your first line, claiming a daily streak, or using a power-up — together with the score or level involved To find where the game is confusing or unfair and fix it
Crash and performance data Crash reports with the technical details of what went wrong, app start time and screen performance To fix crashes and slowness

We do not collect the contents of your messages, contacts, photos, files, or anything else on your device. The App requests no permissions that can read your personal content.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress — high score, coins, gems, owned themes, daily streak, and your sound, music and vibration preferences — is stored locally on your device only (using Android SharedPreferences). This progress is never uploaded, is not accessible to us, and is removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Who processes this data

The data described in section 1 is processed by Google on our behalf, as part of the Firebase service, and is governed by Google's privacy terms for Firebase.

We do not sell, trade or share this data with any other third party, and we have switched off Firebase's optional data-sharing settings, so it is not contributed to Google's benchmarking or product-improvement programmes.

4. How long it is kept

Firebase Analytics data is retained for 2 months, the shortest retention Google offers. Crash reports are retained for up to 90 days. Aggregated statistics that cannot identify an installation may be kept longer.

5. Your choices

You can stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the App, which also deletes the installation identifier. You may also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalisation in your Android Settings under Privacy.

If you would like the data associated with your installation deleted, contact us at the address in section 9 and we will do so.

6. Advertising

The App does not display advertisements and contains no in-app purchases. If a future version introduces either, this policy will be updated and published before that version is released.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The data described above is limited to pseudonymous device and gameplay information and is not used to build advertising profiles. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
